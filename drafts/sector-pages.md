## 1. Investment firms

**SEO title:** Websites and brands for investment firms | Pixel Silo
**Meta description:** Brand, website and imagery for investment platforms, asset managers and private capital firms, where allocators judge the firm before the first call.

**Eyebrow:** [ 00 ] Investment firms

**H1:** Allocators read the site
*(muted line)* before they read the deck.

**Intro (column 2, main):**
An investment firm is judged on how it runs before anyone asks for the numbers. The website is often the first thing an allocator, a manager or a candidate sees, and it is read with the same care as the track record.

**Intro (column 3, detail):**
So the work starts with strategy: who has to be convinced, what they need to believe, and what the site has to prove before a meeting is ever booked. The brand, the website and the imagery are then made together in one studio, by the person you brief.

### What the site has to prove

**01 · That the firm is solid**
Capital goes where it feels safe. The site has to show the structure, the process and the permanence behind the name.

**02 · How it actually works**
Platforms, strategies and mandates are hard to explain in a sentence. The site should make the model clear to an allocator in a minute and to a specialist in five.

**03 · That the people are worth backing**
Investors back teams. Managers and candidates are choosing you too. The site has to speak to all three without diluting the message for any of them.

**04 · That it keeps up with the firm**
Firms add strategies, people and offices. The site has to change with them, so it is built for your team to update and, if you want, for us to carry forward with Adapt.

### The work

**Eschler Asset Management** · Alternative investment, multi-manager platform
Eschler gives independent portfolio managers the regulated foundation to run their strategies, and gives investors institutional access to that talent. After more than a decade in business, the site said almost nothing about how the platform worked, just as the firm was expanding. We built a brand on stone, a permanent base with a sharp vein for the manager's edge, then a site where that idea becomes the architecture of every page.

**Torre Search** · Executive search for private capital
Torre builds leadership teams for private capital investors and the businesses they back. As a new firm with no history to point to, it had to earn the trust of investors and founders straight away. We created the brand and a site built around one idea: the circle in the O of Torre, a search being narrowed down.

### How we work with investment firms

- **One person from brief to launch.** The person you brief designs, builds and makes the imagery. Nothing is relayed second hand.
- **A fixed scope and price before design starts.** You know the cost and the timeline before any design begins.
- **Your compliance team stays in charge.** We work alongside them on wording and approvals. Regulatory responsibility stays with you.
- **Discreet by default.** Video updates, calls or in person, whichever suits how your firm runs.

## 2. Property

**SEO title:** Websites and brands for property developers and agents | Pixel Silo
**Meta description:** Websites, brands and imagery for developers, new homes sales and prime property advisers, built to sell a home before anyone visits.

**Eyebrow:** [ 00 ] Property

**H1:** Buyers decide from the site
*(muted line)* long before the viewing.

**Intro (column 2, main):**
In property, the site does the first viewing. Buyers, developers and agents judge the scheme, the area and the people behind it from a screen, often before they have spoken to anyone.

**Intro (column 3, detail):**
So the work starts with strategy: who the site is for, what they need to believe, and how each visit ends in a registration or an enquiry. The brand, the website and the imagery are then made together in one studio, so every development can have its own identity without starting from scratch.

### What the site has to prove

**01 · That the home is worth seeing**
Buyers need to picture living there. Imagery, motion and the order of the page do more of the selling than any paragraph.

**02 · That the area is right**
A scheme sells its surroundings too. Maps, local places and the feel of the neighbourhood belong on the site, not just in the brochure.

**03 · That the seller can be trusted**
Developers choosing an agent, and buyers choosing a home, are both looking for reasons to rule someone out. The site should leave them none.

**04 · That it scales to the next scheme**
Every development needs its own identity. The site should let your team launch the next one through the CMS, with its own pages, plots and enquiries.

### The work

**Regenta** · New build sales and marketing
Regenta sells new homes for developers, housing associations and local authorities, so the site has to win over developers and buyers at once. Each development has its own CMS page with its own colours, imagery, filterable plots, floorplans and a map, and enquiries go straight into the CRM. When SiteSales became Regenta, we were asked back for a full rebuild.
